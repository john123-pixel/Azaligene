Inside the companion

Everything in the book, made to be remembered.

Reading a guide once is a start. This app helps the important parts stay with you — in short, gentle sessions that fit around a busy life.

Flashcards

Cards that flip in four different ways

Pick the motion you like best. Rate each card honestly, and smart review brings back the ones you missed first.

The question book

A practice book you actually turn

All 300 questions, laid out as pages. Choose an answer and the solution is written on the facing page. Choose how the pages turn: a soft curl, a classic page or a lifted flutter.

Six timed practice tests

Sit a full 50-question test with an answer sheet and no peeking, then review every miss, page by page.

Meet Maple, your guide

A small, warm companion who shows you around, cheers your progress and offers study tips when you need them.

Read aloud & large text

Have any card or question read to you, switch on larger text, and study in light or dark mode.

Search everything

Find any card or question in a moment. Press / anywhere.

Streaks, goals & badges

A daily goal, an activity calendar and quiet milestones to mark how far you have come.

Bookmarks & quick exams

Mark pages to revisit with a ribbon, and run a quick self-graded exam from the flashcards at any time.

Twelve chapters

One calm plan, chapter by chapter.

Every card and question points back to its section in the book, so you always know where to read more.

Written for all of Canada

Thirteen sets of rules. Notes for yours.

Most cards apply everywhere. Cards that only apply in one province are labelled, and you can filter to your own.

Private by design

Your plan is personal. So is your progress.

Stays on this device

Your name, ratings and scores are kept only in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, and no advertising cookies are used.

Yours to keep or delete

Export a backup file to move between devices, or erase everything with one button in Settings.

Education, not advice

A study aid for the book. Laws differ by province and change — confirm with a lawyer or notary before you act.
